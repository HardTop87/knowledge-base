## What is CoCoCo?

CoCoCo — short for Connect, Combine, Collaborate — is an open integration platform built for the print industry. It connects your machines, people, and production data in one place, and lets you automate the workflows that keep your operation running.

At its core, CoCoCo gives you three things:

**Visibility** — You see what's happening across your production floor in real time. Which machines are running, which jobs are in progress, where things are stuck.

**Integration** — CoCoCo connects to your existing machines and systems via standard protocols (MQTT, HTTP, JMF, SQL). No rip-and-replace. It works with what you already have.

**Automation** — You define workflows that react to events automatically. A job comes in, gets validated, routed to the right machine, and tracked through to completion — without manual intervention.

## Who is it for?

**Admins** set up the platform — inviting team members, managing access rights, connecting machines, and configuring workflows. See [Inviting Team Members](#inviting-team-members) and [Understanding IAM](#understanding-iam).

**Production managers** use dashboards and tools to monitor jobs, handle exceptions, and keep things moving.

**Shopfloor staff** interact with the platform at terminals to report progress and receive job instructions. See [Building a Kiosk App](#kiosk-app).

## How it's structured

**Users, Teams & Policies** — who has access and what they can do. See [Understanding IAM](#understanding-iam).

**Networks & Devices** — how your machines connect to the platform. See [Protocols Explained](#protocols-explained).

## The building blocks at a glance

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Job** | A production order moving through your operation |
| **Work Center** | A machine, person, tool, or location that produces work |
| **Device** | A connected machine sending data to CoCoCo |
| **Workflow** | An automated process triggered by events |
| **Policy** | A set of access rules attached to a user |
| **Custom App** | A bespoke interface built on the platform |
