## What are Capabilities?

Capabilities describe what a Work Center can do — the job types it can handle, media formats it supports, and any production-specific attributes. They are used by the production planning tools to route jobs to the right machine.

## How to assign Capabilities

1. Go to **Data → Work Centers**
2. Open the Work Center you want to configure
3. Go to the **Capabilities** tab
4. Click **+ Add Capability**
5. Select the Capability type and fill in the values
6. Click **Save**

## Managing Capabilities

- Capabilities can be added, edited, or removed at any time
- A Work Center can have multiple Capabilities
- The available Capability types depend on your platform configuration
