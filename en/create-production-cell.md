## What is a Production Cell?

A Production Cell is a named group of Work Centers. It represents a functional area of your production floor — for example `Digital Printing`, `Finishing`, or `Binding`.

Production Cells help you organize your Work Centers and make it easier to assign jobs to the right area.

## How to create a Production Cell

1. Go to **Data → Production Cells** (via the top Data menu)
2. Click **+ Add Production Cell**
3. Enter a **Name** (e.g. `Digital Printing`)
4. Optionally add a **Description**
5. Click **Save**

Once created, you can add Work Centers to this cell.
