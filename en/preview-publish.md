## Previewing

While editing, click **Preview** to open a live preview. The preview runs with your credentials and accesses live platform data.

Use the preview to test interactions, check layout, and verify dark/light mode behavior.

## Publishing

When the app is ready:

1. Click **Publish**
2. The app becomes immediately available to users with access

Published apps appear in the appropriate location by Kind:

- **Page** → left sidebar
- **Dashboard** → Dashboards tab list
- **Kiosk** → kiosk device interface
- **Job View** → Job detail panel

## Version history

Every publish creates an immutable version snapshot. Roll back to any previous version instantly if something breaks.

[Version History and Rollback](#version-history)

## Working copy vs. published

The editor always works on the **working copy** — a draft invisible to users until published. Publishing the working copy creates a new version and makes it live.
