## Customer and Supplier records

CoCoCo maintains a shared contact database for customers and suppliers. Each contact can be tagged as one or both.

## How to add a Customer

1. Go to **Data → ERP → Customers**
2. Click **+ New Customer**
3. Fill in company name, contact details, billing address, and payment terms
4. Click **Save**

## How to add a Supplier

1. Go to **Data → ERP → Suppliers**
2. Click **+ New Supplier**
3. Fill in company details and contact information
4. Set the **Currency** if the supplier invoices in a foreign currency
5. Click **Save**

## From each record, you can view

- Full **Order history** or **Purchase Order history**
- Outstanding **Invoices**
- Customer-specific **Price overrides**
