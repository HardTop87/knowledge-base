## Invoice types

| Type | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Invoice** | Standard billing document |
| **Credit Note** | Corrects or cancels a previous invoice |
| **Proforma** | Advance invoice for customs or pre-payment |

## How to create an Invoice

1. Go to **Data → Commercial → Invoices**
2. Click **+ New Invoice**
3. Select the **Customer** and optionally link to an **Order**
4. Add **Line Items** with quantities and amounts
5. Set the **Due date** and **Payment terms**
6. Click **Save as Draft**, then **Issue** when ready

## Creating directly from an Order

1. Open the completed Order
2. Click **Create Invoice**
3. Line items are pre-filled from the Order
4. Adjust if needed and click **Issue**

## Credit Notes

1. Open the original Invoice
2. Click **Create Credit Note**
3. Adjust the line items to reflect what is being credited
4. Issue the credit note

## Invoice status and payment

An Invoice carries its own status as it moves from draft to settled:

**Draft → Sent → Partially Paid → Paid** — and may also be **Overdue**, **Void** (cancelled), or **Credited** (offset by a Credit Note).

Payment is tracked separately, so you can see where money stands at a glance:

**Unpaid → Partially Paid → Paid** — plus **Overdue**, **Refunded**, or **Written Off**.
